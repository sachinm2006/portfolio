Hackathon · Round 1

HackIIITD — Kabadiwala Connect

With team SPRINTERS, I worked on a platform concept to bring informal e-waste collectors into the formal recycling chain by connecting households, local collectors, and authorized recyclers.

22/25 3 points short of the shortlist

The problem

Informal collectors can face unclear pricing, unsafe handling, limited digital records, and poor traceability after collection.

Our proposed solution

  • Camera-assisted e-waste identification
  • Transparent, source-backed price estimates
  • Recycler discovery using the TinyFish API
  • Verified handovers with OTP, photos, and weight records
  • Digital records for traceability through the recycling journey

What I’m taking forward

Our 22-point score fell three points short of the 25-point shortlist for the in-person 24-hour final at IIIT Delhi. The experience strengthened my understanding of problem identification, solution design, technical architecture, API integration, feasibility, and presenting an idea clearly. I’m grateful to teammates Akshay M U and Ganesh H, and I’m looking forward to learning through more startups and hackathons.
